# IT Security Policy (training sample)

This is a sample document for training purposes only. It does not describe any real organisation.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the previous 10 passwords.
Passwords expire every 180 days for standard accounts and every 90 days for administrator accounts.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and every system that holds customer data.

## Laptops and removable media

All laptops use full-disk encryption. USB storage devices are blocked by default; the IT Service Desk can grant
a 7-day exception after the employee's manager approves the request.

## Reporting incidents

Report suspected phishing, lost devices or data leaks to the Security Operations Center within one hour,
by phone (extension 5555) or through the "Security incident" form in the service portal.

## Customer data

Customer data stays on systems inside the company's data center. Copying customer data to personal email,
personal cloud storage or messaging applications is prohibited and is a disciplinary offence.
